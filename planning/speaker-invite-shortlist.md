# PAG34 speaker invitation shortlist

Prepared October 24, 2026. This is a private planning shortlist, not a confirmed speaker program. No invitations have been sent.

Workshop: Genomics of Crop Ecosystem Services, Sunday January 10, 2027, 4:00–6:10 PM PST, Pacific E, San Diego. Organizer must provide names by November 16, 2026. Talks are approximately 20 minutes, in person; the meeting does not cover speaker costs.

Priorities reflect scientific fit and program balance, not likelihood of acceptance. Proposed talk angles below are suggestions, not existing talk titles. Institutional/lab sources substantiate the research connections; attendance, availability, current contact details, and any PAG scheduling conflicts remain to be checked.

## Recommended first round

Start with Busch, DeHaan, Van Deynze, Cregger, Harpur, and Moore, subject to the organizer’s existing commitments and actual available slots. This balances carbon/root biology, perennial grain genomics, orphan crops, microbiomes, pollinators, and cover crops. Six 20-minute talks would use 120 of the 130 minutes, leaving 10 minutes for introductions and transitions; adjust talk length if discussion time is needed.

## Outreach checklist

- Ask whether they already plan to attend PAG34 and are available during the workshop.
- State the in-person format, approximately 20-minute slot, location, and lack of speaker cost reimbursement.
- Request a proposed title and a short description linking genomic results to an ecosystem service.
- If unavailable, ask whether a colleague, postdoc, or advanced student presenting relevant new findings could attend.
- Reconcile responses with the organizer before treating anyone as confirmed or publishing their name.
